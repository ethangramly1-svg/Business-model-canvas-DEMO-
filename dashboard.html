Provenmade exists because "handmade" became a marketing word instead of a fact. The product bet is narrow on purpose: instead of a seller-level badge anyone can apply for once, every single listing carries its own timestamped, on-device-signed capture of the making process — so buyers are judging evidence, not a claim.

Voice notes

  • Plain-spoken and specific — "3–6 timestamped checkpoints," not "rigorous verification process"
  • Honest about limits — states what verification does not prove, every time it comes up
  • Craft-forward, not corporate — sellers are named people with tools and studios, not "vendors"
  • No manufactured urgency — no countdown timers, no fake scarcity messaging
