# Business model canvas — Provenmade

A verified-human-made goods marketplace concept, researched and prototyped end to end: market research, the business model, and an interactive wireframe.

## Contents

- `business-idea-research.md` — ten researched, source-backed online business ideas, plus the full Provenmade deep dive (the problem, the unique verification mechanism, business model, MVP scope, and honest risks).
- `provenmade-wireframe.html` — interactive wireframe mockup. Download and open in a browser, then click the tabs to move between the homepage, listing page, verification certificate, and seller capture flow.

## The idea, in one line

Etsy is losing buyers and sellers to a flood of AI-generated and dropshipped listings. Provenmade is a marketplace where every handmade listing carries cryptographically signed, timestamped proof that a human actually made it — captured during the making process itself, not a self-reported checkbox.
